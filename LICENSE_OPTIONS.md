# License options

The repository currently has no final license decision. This is a practical comparison for Elito's review, not legal advice.

## MIT

Very permissive: reuse, modification, and redistribution are allowed with attribution and the warranty disclaimer. It is simple and broadly compatible, but offers no explicit patent license and does not impose strong share-alike requirements.

## Apache-2.0

Permissive with an express patent license, NOTICE/attribution requirements, and rules around modified files. It is often a good fit for software and research tooling, but downstream dataset and foundation-model terms still need separate review.

## AGPL-3.0-or-later

Strong copyleft for software, including network use obligations in the relevant cases. It can preserve source-sharing expectations, but may be incompatible with some proprietary integrations and does not automatically license model weights or datasets.

Before choosing, review ownership of source code, third-party dependencies, datasets, and the foundation model's terms. Keep `LICENSE_DECISION_REQUIRED.md` until a choice is made.
